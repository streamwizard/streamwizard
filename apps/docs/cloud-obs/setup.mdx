The first time you open Cloud OBS, StreamWizard walks you through this. It takes
a couple of minutes and you only do it once. Here's the whole thing so you know
what's coming.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Make an ingest key">
    This is the private address your phone or encoder streams to. Give it a name
    you'll recognize later, like "phone" or "backpack", and create it. Keep it
    private. Anyone who has it can stream as you. More on [ingest keys](/cloud-obs/ingest).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start your cloud OBS">
    Hit **Launch Cloud OBS**. This spins up your own private copy of OBS in the
    cloud. It's yours alone, nobody else is in there.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for OBS to boot">
    Nothing to click here. OBS starts itself inside your container. Give it about
    10 to 30 seconds. The status badge turns green and says **OBS Connected**
    when it's ready.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your alerts (optional)">
    Want follow, sub, and donation alerts on screen? Paste in your alert link
    (from StreamElements, Streamlabs, or wherever you set them up) and we'll add
    it as a source. You can skip this and do it later.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open OBS and look around">
    Hit **Open OBS** to see your cloud OBS live in the browser, exactly like the
    desktop app. Check your camera, arrange your scenes, and you're set. 🎬
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What you get after setup

Once you're through those steps, your Cloud OBS page becomes your home base
every time you stream:

- A **live preview** of what OBS is showing.
- A **Go live** button to start and stop streaming.
- Your **scenes** as buttons you can tap to switch.
- Live info on how your incoming signal and your OBS are doing.

One thing left before you can stream: your phone doesn't know where to send its
video yet. That's next.

<Card title="Next up: Connect your phone" icon="key" href="/cloud-obs/ingest">
  Copy your streaming URL into your phone app or encoder. Two minutes, tops.
</Card>
