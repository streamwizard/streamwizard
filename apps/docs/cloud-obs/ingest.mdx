Your **ingest key** is where your phone or IRL encoder sends its video. Think of
it as a private mailbox: your camera drops video in, and your cloud OBS picks it
up. It lives on the **Ingest** page in the sidebar.

<Frame caption="The Ingest page: your push URLs up top, your keys down below. The key itself is hidden here.">
  <img src="/images/cloud-obs/ingest-keys.png" alt="Ingest keys page" />
</Frame>

<Warning>
  Your ingest key is a password. Anyone who has it can stream as you. Don't paste
  it in a Discord you don't control, don't show it on stream, and don't put it in
  a screenshot. If it gets out, **rotate** it (see below).
</Warning>

## Getting your streaming URL

Under **Your ingest URLs** you'll see two options. You only need one. Pick the
one your app or gear uses:

- **SRT** works for most phone apps and encoders. Copy this URL and paste it in.
- **SRTLA** is for bonded setups that combine several connections at once
  (Belabox, IRLToolkit, Moblin, and similar). If you're not using one of those,
  you don't need this.

You don't need to understand how SRT and SRTLA work. They're just the two ways to
hand your video to us. Copy the matching one, paste it into your app, start
streaming. That's it.

Tap the copy button next to a URL to grab it. Your key is already baked into the
URL, so there's nothing else to fill in.

## Making and managing keys

You can have more than one key. Handy for labelling your gear ("phone",
"backpack", "backup") or handing a fresh key to a guest.

Each key in **Your keys** has a few buttons:

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Copy** | Copies the key to your clipboard. |
| **Send to Discord** | DMs the key to you on Discord, so you can grab it on your phone. |
| **Rotate** | Makes a brand new key and kills the old one. Use this if a key leaks. |
| **Delete** | Removes a key you're done with. |

<Warning>
  **Rotate** breaks any device still using the old key. After you rotate, update
  the URL in your streaming app or it won't connect.
</Warning>

## Checking your connection

The **Live ingest status** card shows how your incoming video is doing while
you're streaming: whether the connection is good, how much video is coming
through, and whether anything is getting dropped. When you're not streaming, it
just says there's no active stream right now.

If the numbers look rough, that's usually your mobile signal, not us. A weaker
signal means lower quality or a frozen picture. This is exactly what the
[Auto Switcher](/cloud-obs/auto-switcher) is built to cover for you.
