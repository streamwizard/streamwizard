Cloud OBS is StreamWizard's whole streaming setup, running in the cloud instead
of on your computer. Point your phone or IRL backpack at us, and we handle OBS,
your scenes, your overlays, and the stream out to Twitch. Your PC can be off.
You can be on a train.

## How it works

Three pieces do the work. You don't have to think about them much, but here's
the whole trip your video takes:

1. **Your phone or encoder sends video to us.** It pushes to a private address
   called your [ingest key](/cloud-obs/ingest). Works great over mobile data.
2. **Your cloud OBS catches it.** We run a private copy of OBS in the cloud for
   you. Your video shows up inside it, ready to drop into a scene.
3. **Cloud OBS streams to Twitch.** You arrange your scenes and hit
   [Go live](/cloud-obs/controls). The stream goes out from the cloud, not from
   your house.

The point: you carry a phone, we carry everything else.

## New here? Read these in order

These docs follow the same path you'll take as a new user. Go top to bottom and
you can't get lost:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get set up">
    Do the one-time setup: make a key, start your cloud OBS. Five steps, a couple
    of minutes. Start at [Get set up](/cloud-obs/setup).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your phone">
    Copy your streaming URL into your phone app or encoder. That's the
    [Ingest keys](/cloud-obs/ingest) page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Learn your controls">
    Go live, switch scenes, check your stream's health. All on
    [Your controls](/cloud-obs/controls).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up your safety net">
    Turn on the [Auto Switcher](/cloud-obs/auto-switcher) so a dead spot doesn't
    leave your viewers staring at a frozen frame.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Put the remote on your phone">
    Install the [Stream Deck](/cloud-obs/deck) on your home screen and you're
    ready to head out.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Already set up and just looking something up? Jump straight to the page you need
in the sidebar.

## Who this is for

If you stream IRL (out and about, on mobile data) or you just want OBS running
somewhere other than your own PC, this is for you. If you only ever stream from
your desktop with OBS already installed, you probably don't need any of this.
