The **Stream Deck** is StreamWizard on your phone, stripped down to just the
buttons you need mid-stream. Big scene buttons, a Go live button, and not much
else. Built for changing scenes with one thumb while you're walking around.

You reach it at **streamwizard.org/deck** on your phone.

## Put it on your home screen

The deck can live on your phone's home screen like a real app. Open it in your
phone's browser and you'll get an **Install** prompt ("put the deck on your home
screen"). Tap it, and now the deck is one tap away, full screen, no browser bars
in the way.

Do this before you head out. Fishing through browser tabs while you're live is no
fun.

## What's on it

<Frame caption="Scenes as big buttons. Tap one to switch. The live one is highlighted.">
  <img src="/images/cloud-obs/deck-scenes.png" alt="Stream Deck scenes grid" style={{ maxWidth: "360px" }} />
</Frame>

- **Go live** at the top starts and stops your stream. It turns red while you're
  live.
- **Current scene** shows which scene is on air right now.
- **Your scenes** are big buttons in a grid. Tap one to switch to it. The scene
  that's live has a green dot and stays highlighted.

## Switching scenes

Tap a scene button. That's the whole thing. It switches right away, no confirm,
no menu. This is the reason the deck exists: you're on the move, you tap, the
scene changes.

## Holding a scene

Here's the clever bit. If you have the [Auto Switcher](/cloud-obs/auto-switcher)
running, tapping a scene on the deck also **holds** it. That means the Auto
Switcher won't switch you away from it, even if your signal wobbles.

Why that matters: say you stop to chat on a static scene. Your camera feed isn't
moving, so the Auto Switcher might think you dropped signal and cut to
"Connection lost". By tapping the scene on your deck, you tell it "no, stay here,
I meant to do this."

<Frame caption="Tap a scene to hold it. The deck shows what's held and lets you release it.">
  <img src="/images/cloud-obs/deck.png" alt="Holding a scene on the deck" style={{ maxWidth: "360px" }} />
</Frame>

While a scene is held, the deck shows **Holding [scene name]** and says auto
switching is paused. When you're ready to let the Auto Switcher take over again,
tap **Resume auto switching**.

<Tip>
  Tapping a different scene just moves the hold to that new scene. So you can
  walk, tap, walk, tap, and the Auto Switcher stays out of your way the whole
  time.
</Tip>

## If OBS isn't running

If your cloud OBS is off, the deck tells you and gives you a **Start** button to
turn it on right there. If it's still booting, you'll see a little progress
countdown. If the connection drops, the deck reconnects itself when your phone
wakes up or gets signal back, so you're not hunting for a reconnect button.

## No Cloud OBS yet?

If you haven't set up Cloud OBS at all, the deck points you to the dashboard to
get started. Do the [setup steps](/cloud-obs/setup) once on a bigger screen, then
the deck is ready for you on the go.
