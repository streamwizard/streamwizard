When you stream from your phone, your signal will drop sometimes. A tunnel, a
dead spot, a crowded stadium. Without help, your viewers get a frozen picture and
have no idea what's going on.

The **Auto Switcher** watches your incoming signal and, the moment it goes bad,
switches OBS to a scene you picked ("Connection lost", "Be right back", whatever
you like). When your signal comes back, it switches you back to your live scene.
You don't touch a thing.

You'll find it on the **Auto Switcher** tab of your [Cloud OBS page](/cloud-obs/controls).

## Turning it on

Flip the **Auto switcher** toggle on. Then set two things and you're basically
done.

### How many scenes should it manage?

- **2 scenes** is the simple option. You have a **Live** scene and a
  **Connection lost** scene. Your signal is either good enough, or it isn't.
- **3 scenes** adds a middle **Low bitrate** scene, for when your signal is rough
  but still alive. Good if you'd rather show a lower-quality picture than cut away
  completely.

Then pick which of your scenes plays each role, and that's the core of it.

### How twitchy should it be?

Pick how quickly it reacts:

| Setting | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Relaxed** | Waits a bit longer before switching. Fewest false alarms. |
| **Balanced** | The middle ground. The right pick for most setups. |
| **Fast** | Switches almost instantly. For when a single frozen frame is one too many. |

Not sure? Leave it on **Balanced**.

<Tip>
  There's an **Advanced mode** if you want to hand-tune every number yourself.
  Most people never need it. If you're not sure what a threshold is, you don't
  need advanced mode.
</Tip>

## Nice extras

A few optional toggles make the switch smoother for you and your chat:

- **Log switches to your stream events** puts each switch in your activity feed,
  so you can see later exactly when you dropped.
- **Post in Twitch chat when it switches** has the StreamWizard bot tell chat
  what's happening ("signal dropped, back in a sec"). You can edit the messages.
- **Show a warning before switching** flashes a heads-up on screen first.
- **Stop the stream after a long outage** ends the stream for you if you've been
  gone too long, so you're not broadcasting a frozen screen for an hour.

## Holding a scene

Sometimes you want the Auto Switcher to leave you alone. Maybe you're parked, on
wifi, or just chatting on a static scene and you don't want it second-guessing
your signal.

That's what **Hold a scene** does. Pick a scene, choose how long to hold it (15
minutes, an hour, or until you release it), and hit **Hold scene**. While a scene
is held, the Auto Switcher stands down and won't switch away from it.

<Frame caption="Holding a scene pauses the Auto Switcher until you release it.">
  <img src="/images/cloud-obs/auto-switcher-hold.png" alt="Holding a scene" />
</Frame>

When you're ready to let it do its job again, hit **Release**.

<Tip>
  On the [Stream Deck](/cloud-obs/deck), holding is even quicker: just tap a
  scene and it holds automatically. One thumb, done.
</Tip>
