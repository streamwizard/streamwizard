The deck lives at [streamwizard.org/deck](https://streamwizard.org/deck). It's
built for a phone in one hand while the other holds a gimbal: big touch
targets, no zooming, no tiny menus.

## Install it as an app

Open `/deck` in your phone browser and accept the install prompt, or use your
browser's "Add to home screen". It launches full-screen like a native app, no
address bar eating your buttons.

## What's on it

- **Go live / End stream**: one big button. It starts and stops the Twitch
  stream from your cloud OBS.
- **Current scene**: what your viewers see right now.
- **Scene tiles**: tap to switch. Any scene whose name starts with `_` or
  `-` is treated as internal and hidden here, which is why `_alerts` and
  `_incoming-sources` never clutter your tiles. Same rule on the dashboard.
  Name your own helper scenes that way and they'll stay out of the way too.
- **Stream status**: your incoming feed's health at a glance.

If your container is off, the deck offers to start it. If you've never set up
cloud OBS, it sends you to [the setup](/irl/cloud-obs) instead. Do that setup
on a laptop or desktop before you head out; the deck is built for running
your stream, not for first-time setup on a phone screen.

## Switching scenes

Tap a tile, your cloud OBS switches. That's the whole thing, and it's the
reason the deck exists: BRB when you walk into a shop, back to IRL when
you're out.

With the [auto switcher](/irl/auto-switcher) running, tapping a tile also
tells it to stand down, so it won't drag you back to a different scene two
seconds later. The deck shows a card with what you're holding and a button
to release it. Release, and the switcher takes over again once your
connection looks good.

<Frame caption="A held scene with its release control.">

The bottom bar's second tab has the switcher's sensitivity presets, for when
you move from open streets into a crowded venue. Everything else about the
switcher lives on the desktop dashboard; mid-stream is not the moment to
redesign your scene logic.
