Your ingest key is how StreamWizard knows an incoming feed is yours. You paste
it into your encoder, point that encoder at one of the two URLs here, and your
IRL feed arrives in your cloud OBS.

<Warning>
Your stream key is a password. Anyone who has it can stream as you. Don't show
it on stream, don't paste it in Discord, and rotate it the moment you think it
has leaked.
</Warning>

## Before you start

- Ingest keys are part of cloud OBS. Without it, **IRL** doesn't appear in the
  sidebar.

## Make your key

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the ingest page">
    Go to **IRL → Ingest**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name it and create it">
    Give it a label so you recognise it later, like `Belabox` or `phone`, then
    select **Create key**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy your settings">
    Three fields appear: an **SRT URL**, an **SRTLA URL** and a **Stream ID**.
    The Stream ID is your key.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You get one key. That's deliberate: there's nothing to manage and nothing to
mix up. If you need a new one, rotate the one you have.

## Which URL to use

| Your setup | Use |
|---|---|
| One connection, one modem | The **SRT URL**, on port 8888 |
| Bonded, several connections at once | The **SRTLA URL**, on port 5000 |

Belabox, Moblin and IRLToolkit all bond, so they take the SRTLA URL. Both URLs
take the same key.

Most encoders have a separate field for the URL and for the stream ID. Paste
the stream ID into the field called **streamid**. If your encoder only gives
you one box, put them together:

```
srt://your-host:8888?streamid=your-stream-id
```

## Getting the key onto your phone

Typing 64 characters on a phone is miserable, so there's a Discord button next
to your key that sends it to you as a DM. You need your Discord account
[linked](/discord) for it to work.

The DM hides the key behind a spoiler, so you can open it on stream without
showing it. It arrives automatically when you first create a key, too.

## Watching the feed come in

**Live ingest status** at the top of the page shows what's actually arriving:

- **Bitrate**, always.
- **RTT**, **packet loss** and a **bandwidth estimate** on SRT and SRTLA.

A badge rates the connection as **good**, **unstable** or **poor connection**
based on round-trip time and loss.

<Note>
The stats update about every two seconds while you're pushing a feed, and clear
to **No active stream right now** if nothing arrives for six seconds. They
don't reconnect on their own, so if the card goes quiet while you know you're
streaming, refresh the page.
</Note>

## Rotating and deleting

**Rotate** replaces the key in place. There's no confirmation, and the old key
stops authenticating straight away, so anything still using it fails the next
time it reconnects. Update your encoder before you next go live.

**Delete** needs a confirmation, because any encoder using that key stops
working immediately and it can't be undone.

## If it doesn't work

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Your encoder won't connect">
    Check the stream ID went into the **streamid** field rather than into the
    URL, and that a bonded encoder is using the SRTLA URL on port 5000 rather
    than the SRT URL on 8888.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Connect Discord in Settings → Integrations first">
    The DM button needs a linked Discord account. See [Discord](/discord).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Everything is disabled">
    If your subscription payment is overdue you can still read the page, but
    creating, rotating and deleting are switched off until it's settled. Copy
    buttons keep working.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The key says Never used">
    Nothing has connected with it yet. That's expected on a key you just made.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
