<Steps>
  <Step title="Copy the overlay URL">
    In the dashboard, open **Overlays** and select **Copy URL** on the overlay's
    card. You can also copy it from inside the editor.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a browser source in OBS">
    In OBS, find the **Sources** box. Select **+**, then **Browser**. Give it a
    name you'll recognize later, like `StreamWizard overlay`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste the URL and match the size">
    Paste the URL into **URL**. Set **Width** and **Height** to your overlay's
    size, 1920 × 1080 unless you changed it. Don't resize it later with the
    transform handles, or it will look soft and land in the wrong place.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn the overlay on">
    Overlays render only while they're active. Flip the switch on the overlay
    card in the dashboard.
  </Step>
</Steps>
