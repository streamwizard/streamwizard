StreamWizard asks Twitch for a set of permissions when you sign in. That set
grows when new widgets need new data. If your account is older than a widget,
your Twitch token predates the permission it needs, and the widget can't load.

You'll see a **Connect Twitch** button in the widget's settings when this
happens. One trip to Twitch and back fixes it for every widget at once.

<Note>
StreamWizard checks your permissions on a schedule rather than on every page
load, so the button can take a little while to appear after a new widget ships.
Signing in again always works, whether or not the button is showing.
</Note>
