The dashboard is your last broadcast in numbers. It's read-only and there's
nothing to set up. Stream something and it fills in.

Before your first stream it says **Nothing here yet**, and that's the whole
page.

## What's on it

### The six stat cards

Time in ads, peak viewers, average viewers, time on air, new follows and new
subs. Each compares itself to your previous stream, so you get
`+12 from last stream` rather than a number with no context.

**Time on air** shows a dash while you're still live, since it needs an end to
measure to.

Turn these off with **Show stream stats** in
[preferences](/account/preferences).

### Viewers over time

Your viewer count across the stream, with markers for clips, subs and follows
laid over it. Select a clip marker to watch that clip without leaving the page.

<Note>
StreamWizard asks Twitch for your viewer count **every five minutes**, so a
four hour stream is about fifty real readings joined up rather than a smoothed
curve. Nothing here is invented between samples.
</Note>

### Best time to stream

Ranks each hour of your stream by viewers and chat activity together, and tells
you which hour won. The **(i)** next to it explains why that hour was picked,
in words, for your specific stream.

Bits are shown per hour but don't count toward the ranking.

### Stats by category

If you played more than one thing, this splits the stream by category with
duration, viewers, follows, subs and bits for each.

Category changes shorter than a minute are ignored, so flicking through the
category picker doesn't produce a row.

### Activity feed

Everything that happened, newest first, with filters for follows, subs, bits,
raids, rewards and updates. Each row shows how far into the stream it was.

While you're live this updates on its own, with no refresh.

### Recent streams

Your last five broadcasts with their peak viewers, and whether that's up or
down on the one before.

### Recent clips

A strip of your newest clips. These are your newest clips overall rather than
only this stream's.

## Editing your stream title and category

The panel on the right isn't read-only. Select **Edit** to change your stream
title and category, and **Save** writes it to Twitch.

This works while you're offline too, in which case it applies to your next
stream. Titles are capped at 140 characters by Twitch.

## How fresh is it

Everything comes from StreamWizard's own record of your stream rather than a
live Twitch read, which is why it works after the VOD is gone.

The activity feed updates live. Everything else is current as of when you
loaded the page, so a live stream's chart gains a point when you reload, not
while you watch.

## If something's missing

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="My first stream isn't here">
    StreamWizard only has streams from after you signed up. There's no
    backfill of broadcasts from before that.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="There's no replay to scrub">
    Twitch didn't keep a VOD for that stream, which happens when archiving is
    off. The stats, clips and activity feed are all still here. See
    [your VODs](/vods/overview).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The chart stopped while I'm still live">
    It advances when you reload, by one point every five minutes.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
