Signing in for the first time gets you a short setup window. Three things to
do, none of them permanent, all of them changeable later in
[Settings](/account/overview).

## Connect your Twitch account

Go to [streamwizard.org](https://streamwizard.org) and select
**Login with Twitch**. You approve StreamWizard from Twitch's own screen. We
ask for the permissions needed to read your clips and run your chat, and
nothing else. You're back in a few seconds.

That's the first of the four steps done before you've seen the dashboard.

## The setup window

<Frame caption="The setup window after signing in, on the clips step.">
  <img src="/images/onboarding/wizard.png" alt="The StreamWizard onboarding window welcoming a streamer by name, offering to sync their Twitch clips." />
</Frame>

It opens over your dashboard and can't be dismissed by clicking away. The bar
along the bottom tracks where you are: **Twitch connected. 1 of 4 done.**

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pull in your clips">
    StreamWizard can already see your Twitch clips. Select **Sync latest clips**
    to pull them in now, or **Skip** and let them arrive on their own.

    Nothing is lost by skipping. StreamWizard syncs automatically while you
    stream, every five minutes and once more at the end, so your library fills
    up during your next broadcast either way. A big channel takes a minute to
    import, so skipping is often the faster route.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Join the Discord">
    Support runs through [Discord](/discord), so this step hands you the invite.
    Once you're in, select **Already joined? Check again** and it picks you up.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Link your Discord account">
    Linking connects your Discord to your StreamWizard account, which gets you
    the go-live post, the Live role while you stream, and tickets that already
    know which account is yours.

    Optional, like the rest. Everything in StreamWizard works without it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Finish with **Take me to my clips** and you land on your clips page.

## Changed your mind later?

Nothing in setup is a one-time decision:

- Clip syncing, stream stats and the Discord switches live in
  [preferences](/account/preferences).
- Linking or unlinking Discord lives in
  [integrations](/account/integrations).
- To pull clips in again, use **Sync** on the [clips page](/clips/syncing).

## Stuck?

Check your Twitch connection in **Settings → Integrations** first, since that
fixes most of it. If a widget later asks you to connect Twitch again, see
[Connect Twitch keeps appearing](/troubleshooting/connect-twitch).

Still broken? Ask on [Discord](https://discord.streamwizard.org).
