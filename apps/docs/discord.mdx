StreamWizard runs its support and its community in Discord. Linking your
Discord account connects it to your StreamWizard account, which gets you
go-live posts, a Live role while you stream, and support tickets that already
know who you are.

Linking is optional. Everything else in StreamWizard works without it.

</Frame>

## What you get

**We post when you go live.** Your title, game and a link land in the
StreamWizard server the moment you go live.

**You show as live.** You get the Live role while you stream, so you sit at the
top of the member list. It comes off when you go offline.

**Support already knows your account.** Open a ticket and we can see which
StreamWizard account is yours, with no "what's your username" back and forth.

**Your ingest key, by DM.** On the IRL ingest page you can have StreamWizard
send your key to you in a Discord DM rather than copying it off a screen you
might be streaming.

## Link your account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Join the server">
    You need to be a member before any roles can be handed out. Join at
    [discord.streamwizard.org](https://discord.streamwizard.org).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Link it">
    Go to **Settings → Integrations** and select **Link Discord**. You'll be
    sent to Discord to approve it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check it took">
    The integrations page shows your Discord username once it's linked. The
    onboarding wizard also walks you through this on your first visit.
  </Step>
</Steps>

We only ask Discord for your account ID. Not your email, not your servers, not
your messages.

## Turn the announcements off

Both are on once you link, and both are yours to switch off in
**Settings → Preferences**:

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Post in the StreamWizard Discord when I go live** | The go-live post. |
| **Show me as live in the StreamWizard Discord** | The Live role while you stream. |

Turning them off doesn't unlink your account, so tickets still know who you are.

## Support tickets

Support happens in Discord rather than over email. In the server, start a
ticket, pick what it's about (bug, feature, support or something else), and you
get a private channel with the team. You'll be sent a transcript when it's
closed.

## Your own voice channel

Join a lobby voice channel in the server (staff pick which ones) and the bot
makes you a voice channel of your own and moves you in. It's yours to run, and
it's deleted when the last person leaves. You don't need to link anything for
this.

The bot pins a list of these commands in your channel's chat, so you don't
have to remember them. Under it are your channel's current settings (owner,
locked or hidden, user limit, who's banned), kept up to date as you change
them, with two buttons: **Invite** and **Reset to defaults**. **Invite** opens
a window with a one-time link to copy for someone outside the server, and a
picker for people who are already in it (up to 5 at once). Only the owner can
use the buttons, and voice staff. Run them while you're in your channel, or from its chat:

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/voice-lock` | Nobody new can join. Whoever is in it now can still come back. |
| `/voice-unlock` | Opens it up again. |
| `/voice-hide` | Only the people in it now can see it. |
| `/voice-reveal` | Makes it visible again. |
| `/voice-rename` | Renames it. Discord allows 2 renames per channel every 10 minutes, and the bot tells you when you can go again. |
| `/voice-limit` | Sets how many people fit, up to 99. 0 removes the limit. |
| `/voice-invite` | Pick a member and they can get in, even when it's locked or hidden. The bot DMs them a link. Leave the member empty and you get a one-time link (good for an hour) for someone outside the server: joining through it gets them in too. Links only work if the server lets you make invites. |
| `/voice-kick` | Disconnects someone. They can come back unless it's locked. |
| `/voice-ban` | Disconnects someone and keeps them from seeing or joining it. |
| `/voice-unban` | Lets them back in. |
| `/voice-transfer` | Hands the channel to someone in it. |
| `/voice-claim` | Takes over a channel whose owner left. |
| `/voice-owner` | Shows who owns it. Anyone can run this one. |
| `/voice-reset` | Puts your channel back to the lobby's defaults (open, visible, nobody banned, the lobby's name and user limit) and forgets your saved settings. The **Reset to defaults** button under the pinned settings does the same. |

Everything except `/voice-claim` and `/voice-owner` is for the owner (and
voice staff, see below). You
can own one channel at a time: join the lobby again and you're moved back to
the one you have.

The bot remembers how you set up your channel: its name, user limit, bans, and
whether it's locked or hidden. Your next channel from the same lobby starts
that way. Each lobby remembers on its own. Staff can turn this off per lobby
with **Remember owner settings** in the dashboard.

Staff pick **voice staff roles** in the dashboard, for the whole server or per
lobby. People with one can't be kicked or banned from a voice channel, always
get in (locked, hidden or full), and `/voice-claim` lets them take a channel
over even while its owner is there. They can also run every owner command and
button on any channel, without owning it: invite, rename, lock, ban, reset and
the rest. They can't ban the owner. What staff change isn't saved as the
owner's settings, and a reset by staff leaves those saved settings alone. When
staff take over or hand over a channel, the old owner stays in as a guest.

Someone picked a bad name? Voice staff (or anyone with Manage Channels) run
`/voice-rename-block` on them. Their channel goes back to the lobby's name, the
name they had saved is forgotten, and they can't rename any voice channel in
the server until staff run `/voice-rename-unblock`. Staff can see and lift
blocks in the dashboard too, under **Blocked from renaming**.

## If something's wrong

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="You're not in the server">
    Your account is linked, but roles can't be given to someone who isn't a
    member. Join the server and your role appears on its own.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Your role didn't arrive">
    The link worked and the role grant didn't. Select **Re-verify** on the
    integrations page to try again.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="You can't unlink">
    If Discord is the only way you sign in, unlinking would lock you out, so
    it's blocked. Add another sign-in method first, then unlink.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
