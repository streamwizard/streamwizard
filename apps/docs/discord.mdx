StreamWizard runs its support and its community in Discord. Linking your
Discord account connects it to your StreamWizard account, which gets you
go-live posts, a Live role while you stream, and support tickets that already
know who you are.

Linking is optional. Everything else in StreamWizard works without it.

  <img src="/images/account/integrations.png" alt="The integrations page showing the Discord section with a Connect Discord button." />
</Frame>

## What you get

**We post when you go live.** Your title, game and a link land in the
StreamWizard server the moment you go live.

**You show as live.** You get the Live role while you stream, so you sit at the
top of the member list. It comes off when you go offline.

**Support already knows your account.** Open a ticket and we can see which
StreamWizard account is yours, with no "what's your username" back and forth.

**Your ingest key, by DM.** On the IRL ingest page you can have StreamWizard
send your key to you in a Discord DM rather than copying it off a screen you
might be streaming.

## Link your account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Join the server">
    You need to be a member before any roles can be handed out. Join at
    [discord.streamwizard.org](https://discord.streamwizard.org).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Link it">
    Go to **Settings → Integrations** and select **Link Discord**. You'll be
    sent to Discord to approve it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check it took">
    The integrations page shows your Discord username once it's linked. The
    onboarding wizard also walks you through this on your first visit.
  </Step>
</Steps>

We only ask Discord for your account ID. Not your email, not your servers, not
your messages.

## Turn the announcements off

Both are on once you link, and both are yours to switch off in
**Settings → Preferences**:

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Post in the StreamWizard Discord when I go live** | The go-live post. |
| **Show me as live in the StreamWizard Discord** | The Live role while you stream. |

Turning them off doesn't unlink your account, so tickets still know who you are.

## Support tickets

Support happens in Discord rather than over email. In the server, start a
ticket, pick what it's about (bug, feature, support or something else), and you
get a private channel with the team. You'll be sent a transcript when it's
closed.

## If something's wrong

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="You're not in the server">
    Your account is linked, but roles can't be given to someone who isn't a
    member. Join the server and your role appears on its own.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Your role didn't arrive">
    The link worked and the role grant didn't. Select **Re-verify** on the
    integrations page to try again.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="You can't unlink">
    If Discord is the only way you sign in, unlinking would lock you out, so
    it's blocked. Add another sign-in method first, then unlink.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
