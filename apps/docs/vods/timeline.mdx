description: "Your follows, subs, raids, cheers and clips laid over the recording, so you can jump to the moment instead of scrubbing."
---

The timeline under the player isn't just a scrub bar. Everything that happened
during the stream is marked on it, so finding "the bit where we got raided" is
one click rather than five minutes of dragging.

## What's on the timeline

| On the bar | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Coloured dots** | Something happened: a follow, sub, raid, cheer, clip or marker. |
| **Red stripes** | A muted segment. Twitch muted the audio over copyrighted music. |
| **Amber stripes** | An ad break. |
| **White line** | Where you are in the recording. |

Select a dot to jump to that moment. Hover one to see what it was and when.

<Tip>
Muted segments are worth checking before you clip. A clip that lands inside a
red stripe has no sound, and there's nothing to be done about it afterwards.
</Tip>

## The events panel

The panel beside the player lists the same events as rows, in order, with who
did it and how much. A cheer shows the bits, a raid shows the viewers, a gift
sub shows how many.

Select a row to jump the player to it. Events you've already passed are dimmed,
so you can see at a glance where you are.

Right-click a row, or a dot on the timeline, for more. Clips get
**View Clip**, **View on Twitch** and **Copy URL**, plus
**Add to Folder** so you can file a clip without leaving the VOD. Everything
else gets **Seek Here**.

## Showing less

A busy stream puts a lot on the timeline. **Filters** at the top right controls
what's drawn, and it drives the timeline and the panel together.

Chat messages, channel updates and moderation actions are hidden to start with,
because they'd bury everything else. Follows, subs, gifts, raids, cheers,
shoutouts, channel point redemptions, bans, scene switches, clips and markers
are shown. **Select All** turns on everything the VOD has, including chat.

Each type shows how many of it this VOD has, so you can tell what's worth
turning on.

## Zooming in

Zoom when markers are packed too closely to tell apart.

- **Shift + scroll** zooms around the point you're looking at.
- The **+** and **−** buttons step in and out, up to 20×.
- Drag the bar to pan once you're zoomed in.
- On a touchscreen, pinch to zoom and drag with one finger to pan.

## Stream markers

Markers you made during the stream show as yellow flags, with whatever note you
left on them.

<Note>
Markers are made while you're live, from the [deck](/irl/deck) or chat, not
from this page. Twitch places a marker at the live playhead, so there's no way
to add one to a past broadcast.
</Note>

## If it doesn't work

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The events panel is empty">
    Either nothing is selected in **Filters**, or StreamWizard has no events
    stored for this stream. Streams from before you joined StreamWizard have no
    event history, and the VOD still plays normally.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="There are no dots but the stream was busy">
    Check **Filters** first. If every type is on and it's still empty, the
    events for that stream weren't matched to this VOD, which can happen when
    the VOD was still processing.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The legend doesn't list event types">
    It shows the progress bar, playhead and any muted or ad segments. Per-type
    colours are in the **Filters** list, where each type sits next to its own
    icon.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
