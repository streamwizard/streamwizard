---
title: "Make a clip from a VOD"
description: "Cut a clip out of a past broadcast, for the moment nobody clipped while you were live."
---

Something great happened and nobody clipped it. Open the VOD, drag out the bit
you want, give it a title, and Twitch makes the clip.

## Before you start

- The VOD still has to exist. Twitch deletes them after 14 days, or 60 days on
  Partner, Turbo and Prime. See [your VODs](/vods/overview).
- Clips are between **5 and 60 seconds**. That's Twitch's limit, not ours.

## Make the clip

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the VOD and find the moment">
    Select the VOD from **VODs**. Use the [timeline](/vods/timeline) to jump
    straight to the follow, raid or cheer that marks the moment.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start a clip">
    Select **Create Clip**, or right-click the timeline exactly where you want
    it and choose **Create Clip Here**.

    You get a 30 second selection centred on that point, and the timeline zooms
    in so you can work precisely.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Drag it to fit">
    Drag either handle to change where the clip starts and ends, or drag the
    middle to move the whole thing. The readout under the timeline shows the
    length as you go.

    While you're in clip mode the player loops your selection, so you can watch
    the cut you're about to make.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Title it and save">
    Type a title, then select **Save Clip**. A title is required: clips with
    real titles get found, and "Clip from VOD" does not.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
**Clip Start** next to the player jumps back to the beginning of your selection
without leaving clip mode. Handy after you've dragged the end around.
</Tip>

## After you save

The clip is made on Twitch, and Twitch publishes it in the background. The
confirmation gives you a **View Clip** link that opens it on Twitch for
trimming or renaming.

<Note>
Give it a few seconds. Twitch doesn't finish a clip the instant it's requested,
so a clip opened immediately can look unfinished. It also appears on the VOD's
own timeline as a clip marker once it's done.
</Note>

The clip joins your [clip library](/clips/overview) on the next sync, so you
can put it in folders and use it in the
[clips widget](/overlays/clips-widget) like any other.

## If it doesn't work

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Duration must be between 5 and 60 seconds">
    The selection is outside Twitch's limits. The handles normally stop you,
    but the message is there if a selection gets out of range.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The clip can't start before the stream did">
    A selection near the very beginning of a VOD can run off the front of it.
    Move it later.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Save Clip is greyed out">
    Either the title is empty, or the player hasn't finished loading. Both the
    clip buttons stay disabled until the player is ready.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="It said the clip was created but something failed">
    The clip exists on Twitch but StreamWizard couldn't record it for syncing.
    Check your Twitch clips page: if it's there, it'll come in on the next
    [clip sync](/clips/overview).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
