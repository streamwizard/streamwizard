title: "Your VODs"
description: "Every archived broadcast on your channel, with the stream's own events laid over it."
---

**VODs** lists the past broadcasts Twitch still has for your channel. Open one
and you get the recording with your follows, subs, raids, cheers and clips
marked along the timeline, so you can find the moment instead of scrubbing for
it.

## What's in the list

| Column | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Thumbnail** | Twitch's preview image. |
| **Title** | The stream title, with a red **Live** badge if that broadcast is still running. |
| **Duration** | How long the stream was. |
| **Views** | Views on the VOD, abbreviated past a thousand. |
| **Created** | When the stream started. |
| **Language** | The channel language at the time. |
| **Type** | Always `archive` here. |

Select any row to open it.

<Note>
Only **archived broadcasts** are listed. Highlights and uploads you made on
Twitch aren't shown, because StreamWizard asks Twitch for archives
specifically.
</Note>

## Twitch deletes VODs on a timer

This is the thing worth knowing before you plan around VODs.

| Account | Twitch keeps VODs for |
|---|---|
| Standard | 14 days |
| Partner, Turbo or Prime | 60 days |

The list comes from Twitch live rather than from anything StreamWizard stores,
so a VOD that has aged out simply stops appearing, and its page reads
**VOD Not Found**. StreamWizard doesn't warn you before that happens.

<Warning>
If a VOD matters to you, clip it or download it before it expires.
Your stream's events and your clips stay in StreamWizard after the VOD is gone,
but the recording itself is Twitch's and StreamWizard can't get it back.
</Warning>

## Moving through the list

A hundred VODs load at a time. **Previous** and **Next** page through the rest.
It's a running sequence rather than numbered pages, so you move one step at a
time.

**Refresh** re-asks Twitch. Use it if you've just finished a stream and the new
VOD isn't listed yet.

## Deleting VODs

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select them">
    Tick the box on each VOD you want gone. The box in the header selects
    everything on the current page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Delete">
    Select **Delete Selected**. The dialog lists every title it's about to
    delete so you can check before confirming.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
Deleting a VOD can't be undone, and it deletes it on Twitch, not just in
StreamWizard. Clips already made from it survive.
</Warning>

Twitch allows five per request, so the button won't run with more than five
selected. Changing page clears your selection.

## If something's wrong

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Failed to load videos">
    Usually your Twitch connection. Open **Settings → Integrations** and check
    Twitch is connected, and see
    [Connect Twitch keeps appearing](/troubleshooting/connect-twitch) if your
    account predates the VOD features.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="VOD Not Found when opening one">
    Either Twitch has deleted it on the timer above, or you deleted it. Both
    look the same from here.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A stream I did isn't listed">
    A stream only has a VOD if you had archiving turned on in Twitch when you
    went live. StreamWizard still tracks the stream and its events either way,
    but without a VOD there's no recording to open.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
