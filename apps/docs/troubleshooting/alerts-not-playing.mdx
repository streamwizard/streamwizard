## First, prove the widget works

<TestEvents />

Fire a test alert in **Local** mode. If the alert plays in the editor, the
widget is fine and the problem is between StreamWizard and OBS. If it doesn't
play in the editor either, the problem is in the widget's settings.

## The alert never fires

**Is the alert switched on?** The alert box lists every alert with its own
toggle. The template switches on follows, subs, cheers and raids and leaves the
other 19 off.

**Is there a minimum set?** Under **Timing**, an alert can require a minimum
before it fires, like a number of bits or raid viewers. A cheer below your
minimum is ignored on purpose.

**Is a variation stealing it?** A variation plays instead of the base alert
when its conditions match. If a variation is misconfigured, it can swallow
alerts you expected to see. Open the alert, check **Variations**, and use the
play button on each one to test it directly.

**Is the overlay switched on?** See [overlay is blank](/troubleshooting/overlay-blank).

## The alert fires but there's no sound

OBS doesn't route browser source audio to your stream by default.

Right-click the browser source, select **Properties**, and turn on
**Control audio via OBS**. The source then appears in your audio mixer, where
you can check it isn't muted and the volume is up.

Also check the alert's own volume under **Media and sound**, and that the sound
file is still in your [media library](/overlays/media-library).

<Note>
**Control audio via OBS** is also the fix for clips that won't autoplay with
sound, because audio routed through OBS isn't subject to the browser's autoplay
rules.
</Note>

## Alerts vanish when I switch scenes

Turn off **Shutdown source when not visible** in the browser source properties.

With it on, OBS closes the page whenever the scene isn't showing. Alerts that
fire while the scene is hidden arrive at a page that isn't running, and they're
gone. They're not queued for when you come back.

## Several alerts fired at once and I only saw some

Alerts queue rather than overlap, so three follows at once play one after
another. The queue holds 50 by default and drops anything past that, so a
follow-bot wave can't book your alert box for an hour. **Most alerts waiting**
changes the limit.
