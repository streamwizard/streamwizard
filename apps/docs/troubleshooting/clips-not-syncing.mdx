title: "Clips aren't syncing"
description: "New clips missing from your library, or the Sync button refusing to run."
---

## You can sync again at…

Syncing is limited to once an hour. Select **Sync** inside that hour and you
get a message telling you the time you can sync again.

This isn't a punishment, it's Twitch's rate limits. Pulling a whole clip
library is a lot of requests, and the cap keeps StreamWizard inside what Twitch
allows for everyone.

If you're waiting on one specific clip, it's usually faster to wait for the
hour than to keep trying.

## New clips aren't appearing on their own

StreamWizard syncs for you while you're live: every five minutes, and once
more when the stream ends. That's the preference in **Settings → Preferences**
reading **Sync Twitch clips automatically**, and it's on by default.

With it off, your library only updates when you select **Sync** yourself.

Note that automatic syncing only runs **while you're live**. Clips someone
makes from your VOD hours after the stream are picked up by your next manual
sync or your next stream, not within five minutes.

## A clip from just now is missing

Twitch takes a little while to make a new clip available through its API, so a
clip made seconds ago can be missing from a sync that ran immediately after.
It arrives on the next sync.

## Clips are there but a folder looks empty

Folders are something you fill yourself. Syncing adds clips to your library,
not to any folder.

Open a clip and use **Add to folder**, or see
[organize clips into folders](/clips/folders). An empty folder shows a help
panel explaining this.

## Nothing syncs at all

Check **Settings → Integrations** shows Twitch as connected. If it offers
**Connect Twitch** or **Reconnect**, your token has gone stale: see
[Connect Twitch keeps appearing](/troubleshooting/connect-twitch).

## Still stuck

Ask in the **#help** channel on our [Discord](https://discord.streamwizard.org),
or [open a ticket](/troubleshooting/contact-support). Tell us roughly when the
clip was made and whether it shows on your Twitch clips page.
