Support runs through Discord rather than email. You get a private channel with
the team instead of a thread that goes quiet for two days.

## Open a ticket

<Steps>
  <Step title="Join the Discord">
    [discord.streamwizard.org](https://discord.streamwizard.org). Select
    **Create Ticket**, which sits in its own channel and is hard to miss.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick what it's about">
    **Bug**, **Feature**, **Support** or **Other**, then say what broke or what
    you want.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Talk to us in the private channel">
    You get a channel with just you and staff. It stays open until the thing is
    sorted, and you're sent a transcript when it closes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
[Link your Discord account](/discord) first and we can see which StreamWizard
account is yours, which skips the "what's your username" round trip.
</Tip>

## What to include

The first reply is much more useful when you say:

- **What you expected, and what happened instead.** "My follower alert didn't
  play" beats "alerts are broken".
- **When it happened**, roughly, and whether you were live.
- **Which overlay and which widget**, by name.
- **What you already tried** from these troubleshooting pages.
- **A screenshot**, if it's something you can see.

For anything involving OBS, also tell us:

- Your **OBS version** (Help → About).
- Whether you're using OBS on your own computer or **cloud OBS**.
- Your **operating system**.

<Warning>
Don't paste your overlay URL, your stream key, or any ingest key into a
screenshot or a message, including in a ticket. Anyone who sees them can use
them. If we need one, we'll ask you for it privately and tell you how to send
it.
</Warning>

## Before you open a ticket

Most reports we get are one of these, and all of them have a page:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Overlay is blank in OBS" href="/troubleshooting/overlay-blank">
    Usually the overlay being switched off, or a stale cache.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Alerts aren't playing" href="/troubleshooting/alerts-not-playing">
    Including alerts with no sound.
  </Card>
  <Card title="A widget shows nothing" href="/troubleshooting/widget-shows-nothing">
    Often correct behaviour rather than a fault.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Clips aren't syncing" href="/troubleshooting/clips-not-syncing">
    The once-an-hour limit catches most people.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Feature requests and ideas

Same place, pick **Feature**. Telling us what you were trying to do matters
more than telling us what to build: the problem usually has a better answer
than the one we'd have guessed at.
