StreamWizard keeps its own copy of your clip list so it can search, filter and
organise them. Syncing is how that list gets filled.

<Frame caption="The filter bar above your clips, with Sync on the right.">
  <img src="/images/clips/sync-button.png" alt="The clips filter bar showing Search, Category, Filters and a Sync button." />
</Frame>

## It mostly happens on its own

While you're live, StreamWizard pulls new clips **every five minutes**, and
once more when the stream ends. That's the preference in
**Settings → Preferences** reading **Sync Twitch clips automatically**, and
it's on unless you turned it off.

So for clips made during your stream, you rarely need to do anything.

<Note>
Automatic syncing only runs **while you're live**. A clip someone cuts from
your VOD the next afternoon isn't picked up five minutes later. It arrives on
your next stream, or when you sync by hand.
</Note>

## Syncing by hand

Select **Sync** in the filter bar above your clips. It reads your whole clip
history from Twitch, so it also refreshes view counts and titles on clips you
already had.

## Once an hour

A manual sync is limited to once an hour. Inside that hour you get:

> Sync skipped. Last sync was less than an hour ago. You can sync again at 14:30.

This is Twitch's rate limits rather than ours. A full history pull is a lot of
requests, and the limit keeps StreamWizard inside what Twitch allows across
everyone using it.

<Warning>
The limit also applies to the sync at the end of your stream. If you hit
**Sync** by hand in the last hour of a stream, the end-of-stream sync is
skipped, silently. That sync is the one that catches up on anything the
five-minute pulls missed, so it's worth leaving it alone while you're live.
</Warning>

The five-minute pulls while you're live are not affected by the limit, and
don't consume it.

## What syncing does and doesn't do

| Syncing does | Syncing doesn't |
|---|---|
| Add clips that are new to StreamWizard | Put anything into a folder |
| Update view counts, titles and featured status | Download anything |
| Link clips to the VOD they came from, when that VOD is known | Create clips |

Folders are something you fill yourself. See
[organize clips into folders](/clips/folders).

## If it doesn't work

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Couldn't load your clips. Check your Twitch connection.">
    Open **Settings → Integrations** and check Twitch is connected. If your
    account is older than the clip features, see
    [Connect Twitch keeps appearing](/troubleshooting/connect-twitch).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Your session ran out">
    Reload the page and try again.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A clip from a minute ago is missing">
    Twitch takes a little while to make a new clip available to other apps, so
    a sync run seconds after the clip was made can miss it. The next one gets
    it.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
