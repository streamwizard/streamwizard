---
title: "Download a clip"
description: "Get an MP4 out of StreamWizard, landscape for YouTube or portrait for shorts."
---

Any clip in your library can be downloaded as an MP4, in landscape or portrait.
Portrait is the one you want for TikTok, Reels and Shorts.

## Download one

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the clip">
    Select a clip to open it, or use the **⋯** menu on its card without opening
    it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a shape">
    Select **Download**, then **Landscape** or **Portrait**. From the card
    menu they're **Download Landscape** and **Download Portrait**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for it">
    StreamWizard asks Twitch for the file and passes it to your browser. It
    saves as `clip title-landscape.mp4` or `clip title-portrait.mp4`.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Portrait is Twitch's own crop, made for vertical video. StreamWizard doesn't
re-frame it, so what you get is what Twitch produces.

## Only your own channel

Downloads work for clips from channels that have connected StreamWizard. That's
your own channel, and anyone else's who uses StreamWizard.

A clip from a channel that never authorized StreamWizard can't be downloaded.
Twitch refuses to sign the file, and there's no way around it from here. You'll
see a 401 error if you try.

## If it doesn't work

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="There's no Download button on this clip">
    The button is hidden when StreamWizard doesn't know which channel the clip
    belongs to, which happens with older or partially synced rows. Run a
    [sync](/clips/syncing) and look again.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="No portrait download URL available">
    Twitch doesn't have a vertical version of that clip. Older clips often
    don't. Landscape still works.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Twitch API error: 401">
    The clip is from a channel that hasn't connected StreamWizard. See above.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Clip not found">
    The clip isn't in your StreamWizard library, only on Twitch. Sync first.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
