---
title: "Goals"
description: "Your Twitch follower, sub and Bits goals on stream as live progress bars."
---

The goal widgets show the goals you run on Twitch. Make a goal in your
Twitch Creator Dashboard, and the bar on your overlay fills as followers,
subs and cheers come in. No refreshing, no typing numbers in by hand.

## Pick your goal

In the overlay editor, open **Widgets → Goals**:

- **Follower goal**: new followers toward your follower goal.
- **Sub goal**: your sub goal, whichever kind you run. Twitch runs one sub
  goal at a time and lets you pick how it counts under **Sub Count**:

| Twitch's Sub Count | What it counts |
|---|---|
| Total Subs | All your active subs. Goes down when a sub runs out. |
| Total Sub Points | The same, weighted by tier: Prime and tier 1 are 1 point, tier 2 is 2, tier 3 is 6. |
| New Subs | Subs bought while the goal runs, starting at 0. Never goes down. |
| New Sub Points | New subs, weighted by tier. |

The widget follows whatever you pick and labels the numbers to match, like
`1 / 10 subs` or `8 / 50 sub points`.

- **Bits goal**: your Bits goal. On Twitch it counts either Bits cheered or
  cheerers (each person who cheers counts once). Both start at 0 when the goal
  starts. The widget follows whichever you pick: `1,200 / 5,000 Bits` or
  `8 / 25 cheerers`.

Twitch runs one goal per kind at a time, so you can have a follower, a sub and
a Bits bar on screen together. Add a widget for each.

## Making and changing goals

Goals live on Twitch. Twitch doesn't let other apps create or edit them, so
you start, change, and end goals in your Creator Dashboard. **Manage goals on
Twitch** in the widget settings takes you there. The widget picks up the
change on its own.

If there's no active goal of its kind, the widget shows nothing on stream.
In the editor it tells you so.

## Reconnect Twitch (accounts from before goals)

Reading goals needs a Twitch permission StreamWizard didn't ask for before.
If you connected Twitch before the goal widgets existed, the widget settings
show a **Connect Twitch** button. One trip to Twitch and you're set. Until
then the bar can't load or update.

## Designs

Pick a look under **Design**. Every goal widget can use every design, and
switching resizes the box to fit.

| Design | What it looks like | Good for |
|---|---|---|
| **Bar** | Title and numbers over a rounded bar | The all-rounder |
| **Strip** | One line of text with a thin line of progress under it | Tickers, corners, tight spots |
| **Ring** | A circle filling up, the current number big in the middle | Next to your camera |
| **Blocks** | The bar in segments that light up, like a health bar | Small goals: with 20 or fewer, every follow or sub gets its own block |
| **Tube** | A thermometer filling from the bottom | Side columns |
| **Arcade** | A pixel XP bar with LEVEL UP when you hit the goal | Games, obviously |
| **Liquid** | A card of water that never sits quite still. New follows, subs or Bits pour in from a tap above, and bigger jumps pour longer | When the goal is the star |

Arcade switches the font to Press Start 2P when you pick it. Change it back
if you like.

## See it before you go live

The settings have four test buttons: **Start**, **Progress**, **Almost
there** (90%) and **Reached**. They play a fake goal on the canvas, or on
your live overlay when the demo bar is set to Live. After a test, **Back to
your Twitch goal** puts the real numbers back. A Live test on your stream
clears itself after 15 seconds.

## Settings

- **Title**: leave it empty to use the goal's name from Twitch, or type your
  own. If neither has one, the widget shows no title.
- **When the goal ends**: keep the full bar up, or hide it a few seconds
  after the goal ends.
- **Show**: title, numbers (`120 / 500 subs`), percentage, how many to go
  (`12 subs to go`), and an icon: a heart for followers, a star for subs, a
  gem for Bits, or your own image.
- **Colors**: a solid fill or a two-color gradient, the track behind it, the
  text, and corner radius.
- **Text**: font, weight, size, and a shadow that keeps it readable over
  bright games.
- **Motion**:
  - **Flash on progress**: a short glow each time the number goes up.
  - **When the goal is reached**: shine, glow, confetti, or nothing.
  - **Goal starts** and **Goal hides**: fade, slide, pop or shrink.

If reduced motion is switched on in the system settings of the computer
running OBS, the widget plays it still: no confetti, no pouring or waves, and
fills jump straight to the new number.
