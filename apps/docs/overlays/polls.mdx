---
title: "Polls"
description: "Your Twitch poll on stream, with live votes, a countdown and the winner."
---

The poll widget shows the poll you run on Twitch. Start a poll, and it shows
up on your overlay with the question, every choice and a countdown. Votes
come in live, and when the poll closes the winner lights up. A few seconds
later the widget gets out of the way until your next poll.

## Add it

In the overlay editor, open **Widgets → Polls** and add **Poll**.

## Starting polls

Polls live on Twitch. Start one from Stream Manager or with `/poll` in your
chat. **Start a poll on Twitch** in the widget settings takes you to Stream
Manager. The widget picks up the poll on its own, and if you refresh your
overlay mid-poll, it comes back with the current votes.

When no poll is running, the widget shows nothing on stream. In the editor it
tells you so.

If the settings show a **Connect Twitch** button, StreamWizard can't read
your polls yet. One trip to Twitch and you're set.

## Designs

Pick a look under **Design**. Switching resizes the box to fit.

| Design | What it looks like | Good for |
|---|---|---|
| **Bars** | A bar per choice, filling with its share of the votes | The all-rounder |
| **Columns** | Columns side by side, rising like a live chart | Two or three choices |
| **Donut** | A ring split by votes, the leader's percentage in the middle | Next to your camera |
| **Strip** | One line: the question, who's ahead and the countdown, over a thin split bar | Tickers, corners, tight spots |
| **Race** | A lane per choice, each runner moving toward the finish line. The winner crosses when the poll closes | When chat is really split |

## When the poll closes

The winning choice lights up and the others fade back. On a tie, every tied
choice lights up. The result stays up for as long as **Show the result for**
says (10 seconds by default), then the widget hides.

## See it before you go live

The widget settings have four test buttons: **Start**, **Votes**, **Close
race** and **End**. The same four sit in the **Poll** row of the editor's
**Demo** bar. To watch polls come and go, press **Poll** under **Simulate**
in the Demo bar: it runs a 20-second poll, closes it with a winner, pauses,
and starts the next one until you press it again. They play a fake poll on the canvas, or on your live overlay when
the demo bar is set to Live. After a test, **Back to your Twitch poll** puts
the real one back. A Live test on your stream clears itself after 15 seconds.

## Settings

- **Title**: leave it empty to use your poll's question from Twitch, or type
  your own.
- **Show the result for**: how long the winner stays up after the poll
  closes, from 3 to 60 seconds.
- **Show**: title, percentage, vote count and countdown.
- **Colors**: a color for each choice, or **Leader only** to color just the
  choice in the lead and keep the rest muted. Also the track, the text and
  corner radius.
- **Text**: font, weight, size, and a shadow that keeps it readable over
  bright games.
- **Motion**:
  - **Flash on votes**: a short flash on a choice every time it gets votes.
  - **When the poll closes**: a glow, confetti, or nothing. A tie gets no
    celebration.
  - **Poll starts** and **Poll hides**: fade, slide, pop or shrink.

If reduced motion is switched on in the system settings of the computer
running OBS, the widget plays it still: no confetti or flashes, and bars jump
straight to the new numbers.
