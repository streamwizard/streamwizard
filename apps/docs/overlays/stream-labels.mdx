A label is one line of live information about your channel: your newest
follower, your follower count, your top cheerer this stream. There's also an
**Event list**, which is a running feed of everything happening at once.

## Before you start

- You have an overlay. [Make one first](/overlays/quickstart) if you don't.

## Add a label

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add the widget">
    In the overlay editor, open **Widgets → Labels**. **Label** gives you a
    single line, **Event list** gives you the feed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick what it shows">
    **Show** under **Content** has nine options, grouped by what they're about.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write the line">
    **Prefix** is the words in front, like `Latest follower`. **Text** is the
    value itself, and takes tokens in braces. Leave **Text** empty for the
    sensible default.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What a label can show

| Group | Label | Shows |
|---|---|---|
| Followers | **Latest follower** | The most recent person to follow. |
| Followers | **Follower count** | How many followers you have. |
| Subscribers | **Latest subscriber** | The most recent sub. |
| Subscribers | **Latest gift** | Who gifted, and how many. |
| Subscribers | **Subscriber count** | How many subs you have. |
| Bits | **Latest cheer** | Who cheered, and how much. |
| Bits | **Top cheerer** | The biggest cheer in the time range. |
| Raids | **Latest raid** | Who raided, and with how many. |
| Channel | **Event list** | A feed of everything below. |

**Text** fills in whatever you put in braces: `{name}`, `{amount}`, and
depending on the label `{tier}`, `{months}`, `{message}`, `{reward}` or
`{event}`. So `{name} - {amount}` prints `MoonPie - 500`. The token buttons
under the field insert them for you.

## Time ranges

**Time range** limits a label to what happened recently: **This stream**,
**Today**, **This week**, **This month**, **This year** or **All time**. Days,
weeks and months follow UTC and weeks start on Monday. **All time** goes back
to when StreamWizard started tracking your channel.

Counts don't have a time range, so **Follower count** and **Subscriber count**
don't offer one.

<Note>
The range applies to the "latest" labels too. **Latest follower** set to
**This week** goes blank every Monday until somebody follows.
</Note>

## The event list

Pick **Event list** and you get two more groups of settings.

**List** sets **Entries** (up to 25), whether it's **Stacked** or **In a row**,
and for a row, a **Separator** and an optional **Scroll like a ticker** with a
**Speed**.

**Events** turns the nine kinds on and off: **Follows**, **New subs**,
**Resubs**, **Gift subs**, **Cheers**, **Raids**,
**Channel point rewards**, **Shoutouts** and **Hype trains**. All are on to
start with. Turn them all off and the list stays empty.

<Tip>
The list is filtered out of your 50 newest events. On a busy stream a rare kind
can show fewer rows than you asked for, because it filtered 25 rows' worth of
follows and found only two raids in the last fifty things that happened.
</Tip>

## Settings

**Text style** has the usual **Font (Google Fonts)**, **Font size**, **Color**,
**Weight**, **Alignment** and **Text shadow**, plus **Prefix color** so the
label and its value can differ.

**Animation** plays when a value changes. **New value comes in with** is
**None**, **Pop**, **Fade in**, **Slide up**, **Slide in from the side**,
**Drop and bounce**, **Typewriter** or **Glow**, with a **Length** in
milliseconds and a **Preview** button.

The animation doesn't replay when the overlay reloads, only when something
actually changes.

## See it before you go live

<TestEvents />

Test events land in the label without being saved. **Clear test events** under
**Testing** puts it back.

In the editor an empty label shows sample data at low opacity so you can place
and size it. On stream it shows **When there's nothing yet** if you set it, and
otherwise nothing at all.

## If it doesn't work

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="My custom text disappeared when I changed the label">
    Switching **Show** resets **Text** to empty, because a template written for
    a cheer rarely makes sense for a raid. The prefix is kept if you wrote it
    yourself.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Layout does nothing">
    **One line** and **Prefix above** only matter when there's a prefix to put
    somewhere. With **Prefix** empty the control is hidden.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The ticker never animates new entries">
    A scrolling row is already moving, so the entry animation is skipped. Turn
    **Scroll like a ticker** off if you want new entries to land with an
    animation.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My follower count is behind">
    Counts refresh every five minutes. Twitch sends no event when someone
    unfollows or a sub lapses, so that refresh is the only way the number comes
    back down.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
