The prediction widget shows the prediction you're running on Twitch: the
outcomes, how channel points are stacking up, a countdown until betting closes,
and the winner once you pick one.

## Before you start

- You have an overlay. [Make one first](/overlays/quickstart) if you don't.
- You start predictions on Twitch, not in StreamWizard. Twitch doesn't let
  other apps create them.

## Add the prediction widget

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add the widget">
    In the overlay editor, open **Widgets → Polls & predictions** and pick
    **Prediction**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check what the panel says">
    The top of the settings tells you what Twitch is reporting right now,
    whether that's a prediction in progress or **No prediction running**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start one to try it">
    Select **Start a prediction on Twitch** to jump to your Stream Manager, or
    use the **Test** buttons to fake one without touching your channel.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Designs

**Design** has five, shared with the [poll widget](/overlays/polls). Picking one
resizes the widget to suit it.

| Design | What it looks like |
|---|---|
| **Bars** | Horizontal bars that fill up. The all-rounder. |
| **Columns** | Vertical bars side by side. |
| **Donut** | A ring split by share. |
| **Strip** | One thin line. Good along the top of a screen. |
| **Race** | Outcomes running at each other. |

## What happens as the prediction plays out

A prediction has more stages than a poll, and the widget follows all of them:

1. **Open.** Viewers spend points. The countdown shows how long is left.
2. **Locked.** Betting has closed and you haven't called it yet. The countdown
   spot reads `Locked`.
3. **Resolved.** You picked the winner. The winning outcome celebrates and the
   result stays up for **Show the result for** seconds, then the widget hides.

**Stay up while locked** controls the middle stage and is on by default.

<Warning>
Turn **Stay up while locked** off and the widget vanishes for the whole locked
stretch. If you lock a prediction and then play a twenty-minute match before
calling it, your viewers see nothing for twenty minutes. Leave it on unless you
have a reason.
</Warning>

A refund never celebrates, whatever **When you pick the winner** is set to.

## Settings

**Prediction**

| Setting | Default | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Title** | Empty | Uses the question from Twitch when empty. |
| **Stay up while locked** | On | See the warning above. |
| **Show the result for** | 10 s | 3 to 60. |

**Show** toggles **Title**, **Percentage**, **Channel points** and
**Countdown**. Channel points is the one that's off by default.

**Colors** has **Each outcome**, giving all ten outcome slots their own colour,
or **Leader only**, which highlights whichever outcome is winning and greys the
rest. Twitch allows up to ten outcomes, though most predictions use two.

**Motion** covers **Flash on points**, which blinks an outcome each time
viewers back it, and **When you pick the winner**, which is **None**, **Glow**
or **Confetti**.

## See it before you go live

<TestEvents />

The **Test** row walks the whole lifecycle: **Start**, **Points**,
**Close call**, **Lock**, **Pick winner** and **Refund**. Select
**Back to your Twitch prediction** when you're done to drop back to real data.

## If it doesn't work

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The widget shows nothing on stream">
    That's deliberate when there's no prediction running. In the editor you get
    a dashed box telling you why instead, so you can still place it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="StreamWizard needs permission to read your predictions">
    <ReconnectTwitch />
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The result was gone when I reloaded the overlay">
    Twitch only reports a finished prediction for 60 seconds after it ends.
    Reload later than that and there's nothing left to show, even with
    **Show the result for** turned up.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Corner radius does nothing">
    **Donut** and **Race** don't have corners to round, so the control is hidden
    for those two. A value you set on another design is kept and ignored.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
