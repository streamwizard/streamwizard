---
title: "Images and video"
description: "Put your own pictures and video on stream: logos, webcam frames, sponsors, animated backgrounds."
---

Three widgets for your own files. **Image** shows one picture, **Video** plays
one video, and **Slideshow** rotates through several pictures in one spot.

All three pull from your [media library](/overlays/media-library), and all three
live under **Widgets → Media**.

## Before you start

- Upload what you want to use to the [media library](/overlays/media-library)
  first, or upload it from the picker while you work.

## Image

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add the widget">
    Open **Widgets → Media** and pick **Image**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a file">
    Select **Add an image** and choose from your media library. PNG, JPG, WebP
    or GIF. A transparent PNG stays transparent, and GIFs animate.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set how it fills the box">
    **Sizing** is **Fit**, **Fill** or **Stretch**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

| Sizing | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Fit** | Shows the whole image, letterboxed if it doesn't match the box. |
| **Fill** | Covers the box and trims what sticks out. |
| **Stretch** | Ignores the image's own shape. |

**Rounded corners** is a percentage, so 100% on a square box makes a circle and
on a wide box makes a pill. It rounds the box, so it works best with **Fill**.

<Note>
Picking a file changes the widget's height to match the picture's shape, keeping
the width you had. That's usually what you want, and it doesn't happen if you've
cropped the item.
</Note>

## Video

Same picker, same **Sizing** and **Rounded corners**, plus playback.

| Setting | Default | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Loop** | On | Off plays once on load and holds the last frame. |
| **Volume** | 0% | Muted. |

MP4 or WebM. A WebM with transparency keeps its transparency on stream, which
is how you do animated overlays without a green screen.

<Warning>
The editor always plays video silently, whatever **Volume** says. You'll only
hear it in OBS. And because browsers block audible autoplay, a video with sound
may come out muted anyway outside OBS.
</Warning>

## Slideshow

Several images taking turns in one spot. Sponsors, socials, your schedule, fan
art.

Add images with **Add your first image**, then **Add another image**. They play
top to bottom, and the arrows on each row change the order. Twenty images is the
limit.

| Setting | Default | Range |
|---|---|---|
| **Time per image** | 5 s | 1 to 120 |
| **Transition** | Fade | **Fade**, **Slide** or **Cut** |
| **Shuffle** | Off | Appears once you have more than two images |

**Shuffle** never picks the same image twice in a row.

<Note>
Only the first image you add resizes the widget. Everything after that fits into
the shape the first one set, so add your most representative image first, or set
the box by hand.
</Note>

## Settings that trip people up

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The widget is invisible on stream">
    A widget with no file chosen shows a dashed placeholder in the editor and
    **nothing at all** on stream. That's by design, but it does mean an
    unfinished widget looks broken rather than empty.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I can't upload an SVG">
    SVGs aren't accepted. They can carry scripts, so allowing them would mean
    anything in your media library could run code on your overlay. Export a PNG
    or WebP instead.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My rounded corners don't follow the picture">
    Rounding is applied to the box, not the image inside it. With **Fit** the
    picture is letterboxed inside that box, so the corners you rounded aren't
    the picture's. Use **Fill**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Shuffle isn't there">
    It only appears above two images. With exactly two, shuffling and
    alternating are the same thing.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The file is too big to upload">
    Files cap at 10 MB each, and your whole library at 100 MB on the free plan.
    The meter at the top of the media library shows what's left.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
