The emote wall puts every emote your chat types on screen, flying across your
stream. Follows, subs, cheers and raids set off a burst of them. It knows your
7TV, BTTV and FFZ emotes as well as your Twitch ones.

## Before you start

- You have an overlay. [Make one first](/overlays/quickstart) if you don't.
- Your 7TV, BTTV or FFZ emotes work automatically if those accounts are
  attached to your Twitch channel. There's nothing to link in StreamWizard.

## Add the emote wall

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add the widget">
    In the overlay editor, open **Widgets → Alerts & chat** and pick
    **Emote wall**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Leave it full screen">
    The wall is added at the full size of your overlay, because emotes should be
    able to go anywhere. If you shrink the box, emotes stay inside it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Try it">
    Select **Send a test message** at the top of the settings to throw a handful
    of emotes across the canvas.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Pick how emotes move

**Animation** under **Chat emotes** sets the style for emotes typed in chat:

| Animation | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Float up** | Drift upward from the bottom. The quiet default. |
| **Rain** | Fall from the top. |
| **Bounce** | Fall and bounce off the floor. |
| **Explosion** | Burst outward from the middle. |
| **Fireworks** | Launch, then scatter. |
| **Spiral** | Curl outward in a spiral. |
| **Zoom** | Rush at the camera. |
| **Slide across** | Travel sideways. |

Each event gets its own animation too, so chat can float up quietly while a
raid sets off fireworks. Select **Preview animation** next to the picker to
watch one without touching chat.

## Set up the bursts

**Events** has a row for each thing that can trigger a burst: **Follow**,
**New sub**, **Resub**, **Gift subs**, **Cheer** and **Raid**. Turn one on and
you get its own animation, an **Emotes** count, and **Which emotes** to pick
what flies.

Leave **Which emotes** empty and the wall uses the emotes from the viewer's own
message, or your channel emotes if there weren't any.

Big events send more. A cheer scales with the bits, a raid with the viewers,
and gift subs with how many were given. It's square-rooted rather than
multiplied, so 400 bits sends about double a 100-bit cheer, not four times as
many. 300 emotes is the hard ceiling for any one burst.

<Note>
Gifted subs arrive from Twitch as a sub for each recipient plus one gift event
for the gifter. The wall deliberately ignores the recipients so you get one
burst instead of twenty. That means turning **Gift subs** off turns off
everything gift related, including the recipients.
</Note>

## Settings

**Look**

| Setting | Default | Range |
|---|---|---|
| **Emote size** | 56 px | 16 to 256 |
| **Time on screen** | 5 s | 1 to 20 |
| **Most at once** | 150 | 10 to 500 |

**Most at once** is the one to turn down if OBS starts stuttering. When the
wall is full, the oldest emotes go first.

**Filters**

| Setting | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Skip !commands** | On | Messages starting with `!` send no emotes. |
| **Cooldown per viewer** | 0 s | How long before the same person's emotes show again. |
| **Blocked emotes** | Empty | Emote codes that never fly. |
| **Hidden accounts** | 8 bots | Logins whose messages are ignored. |

**Hidden accounts** starts with the usual bots already in it: `streamwizardbot`,
`nightbot`, `streamelements`, `streamlabs`, `moobot`, `fossabot`, `wizebot` and
`sery_bot`. Remove one if you want its messages to count.

**Emote sets** under **Chat emotes** toggles **7TV**, **BTTV** and **FFZ**.
Twitch emotes always show, so turning all three off gives you a Twitch-only
wall rather than an empty one.

## If it doesn't work

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Nothing happens when chat types">
    Check that **Show emotes from chat** is on, and that the account testing it
    isn't in **Hidden accounts**. If you're testing with your own bot, note that
    `streamwizardbot` is hidden by default.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A blocked emote still shows up">
    **Blocked emotes** are case-sensitive, the same way chat is. `kappa` will
    not block `Kappa`. Blocking reliably stops an emote coming from chat; an
    emote you picked yourself under **Which emotes** is sent regardless.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My 7TV or BTTV emotes went missing">
    If the provider had an outage, StreamWizard caches the empty result for an
    hour. Your emotes come back on their own once that hour is up.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="OBS stutters while the wall is busy">
    Turn **Most at once** down, then **Emote size**. A 256 px emote at 500 on
    screen is a lot of pixels for OBS to composite every frame.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Note>
If a viewer has reduced motion turned on in their system settings, the wall
sends a third as many emotes and stops them spinning. That's on the viewer's
machine, not yours, so it doesn't change what goes out on stream.
</Note>
