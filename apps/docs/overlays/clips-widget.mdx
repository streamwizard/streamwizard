The clips widget plays your synced Twitch clips on a loop, with captions you
place yourself. It's what most people put on a starting soon or BRB screen.

## Before you start

- You have an overlay. [Make one first](/overlays/quickstart) if you don't.
- You've [synced your clips](/clips/overview). The widget plays from your
  StreamWizard clip library, not from Twitch directly.

## Add the clips widget

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add the widget">
    In the overlay editor, open **Widgets → Media** and pick **Clips**. It
    arrives at the full size of your overlay.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose which clips play">
    **Source Mode** under **Clip Source** is **All Clips** to start with.
    Switch it to **Specific Folders** and tick the folders you want.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the preview">
    The editor plays a sample of your clips right on the canvas so you can see
    the captions land.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Choosing which clips play

| Source Mode | What it plays |
|---|---|
| **All Clips** | Everything in your library. |
| **Specific Folders** | Only clips in the folders you tick. |
| **By Game / Category** | Only clips from the game IDs you add. |
| **Custom Filters** | Folders, games and creators combined. |

**Time Window** narrows it further by when the clip was made, from
**Last 7 Days** through to **All Time**, or a **Custom Range** with your own
dates.

**Sort & Limits** sets the order. It's **Random** by default, which is what you
want for a looping break screen. A clip that just played is kept out of the
draw for ten minutes so you don't see it twice in a row.

<Tip>
Point the widget at a folder rather than at everything. A "best of" folder you
curate makes a far better break screen than a random pick from every clip
you've ever synced.
</Tip>

## Captions

Adding the widget also adds six caption fields as separate layers:
**Title**, **Creator**, **Game**, **Date**, **View count** and **Duration**.
Each one is its own item in the layers panel, so you show, hide, lock and drag
them individually.

**View count** is the one that starts switched off. Turn it on from the layers
panel, not from the widget's settings.

Select a caption to get its own panel, where position and size are percentages
of the clip box rather than pixels. That way the captions stay put if you
resize the widget. You can also just drag them on the canvas.

A caption with nothing to say prints nothing, so a clip with no game recorded
drops the **Game** line rather than showing an empty one.

## Sound

**Muted** under **Playback** is **off** by default, so clips play with sound.

<Warning>
Browsers block autoplaying audio until something on the page has been
interacted with, and an OBS browser source never gets that interaction. If your
clips start silently or refuse to play at all, either turn **Muted** on, or
turn on **Control audio via OBS** in the browser source properties, which is
exempt from the rule.
</Warning>

**Volume** does nothing while **Muted** is on.

## Settings

**Playback**

| Setting | Default | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Muted** | Off | See the warning above. |
| **Volume** | 100% | Ignored while muted. |
| **Clip transition** | Hard cut | Or **Crossfade**. |
| **Crossfade** | 600 ms | 200 to 3000. Only with **Crossfade**. |

**Sort & Limits**

| Setting | Default |
|---|---|
| **Sort By** | Random |
| **Min View Count** | 0 |
| **Featured Only** | Off |

## If it doesn't work

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="No clips match filters">
    The filters are stricter than your library. Widen **Time Window**, drop
    **Min View Count**, or switch **Source Mode** back to **All Clips** to
    confirm clips are syncing at all.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Clips play silently in OBS">
    See the sound warning above. This is a browser rule, not a StreamWizard
    setting.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I changed the folder and nothing happened">
    Folder membership is cached on the overlay for a minute. Give it a moment,
    or refresh the browser source.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Filters I set earlier seem to be ignored">
    Each **Source Mode** only uses its own fields. Game IDs typed while you were
    on **By Game / Category** are kept but ignored once you switch to
    **Specific Folders**.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Note>
**Reset to Defaults** at the bottom of the panel clears everything including
your clip source, not just the look.
</Note>
