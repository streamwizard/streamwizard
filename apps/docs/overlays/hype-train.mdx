When Twitch starts a hype train, a train rolls onto your stream and keeps
riding until the train ends. Everyone who cheered, subbed or gifted subs gets
their own wagon with their name and picture on it. The biggest supporters ride
up front.

## Before you start

- You have an overlay. [Make one first](/overlays/quickstart) if you don't.
- Hype trains come from Twitch. You don't start one, your chat does.

## Add the hype train

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add the widget">
    In the overlay editor, open **Widgets → Alerts & chat** and pick
    **Hype train**. It's added at the full size of your overlay, because a
    bouncing train needs room to roam.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Watch a test run">
    Select **Start a test train**. A fourteen-second train rides through with
    riders arriving as it goes. **Riders**, **Level up** and **End** let you
    push it along by hand once it's running.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick how it moves">
    **Movement** under **Ride** is either **Bounce around**, which sends the
    train ricocheting around the box, or **Straight across**, which runs it
    along the bottom edge.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Who gets a wagon

Everyone who cheers, subs or gifts subs during the train, plus anyone who
chipped in during the five minutes that got the train started. Riders are
ordered by what they gave, and the order re-sorts live, so a wagon hops over
the others when someone overtakes.

Gifted subs credit the gifter, not the recipients. Anonymous contributions are
merged into one rider called `Anonymous`.

**Wagons** caps how many ride individually, at 20 by default. Everyone past
that shares one last wagon showing how many more there are.

## Designs

**Train** under **Look** has three:

| Train | What it looks like |
|---|---|
| **Steam** | A classic locomotive with a smoking chimney. |
| **Neon** | A sleek glowing bullet train. |
| **Toy** | A chunky wooden toy train in bright colours. |

<Note>
Twitch sometimes runs a Golden Kappa train. Those ride in gold whatever colours
you pick here.
</Note>

## Settings

**Ride**

| Setting | Default | Range |
|---|---|---|
| **Speed** | 420 px/s | 100 to 1500 |
| **Faster every level** | 15% | 0 to 50 |
| **Wagons** | 20 | 1 to 50 |
| **New riders arrive** | Drop in | None, Fade, Pop, Chase, Drop in |

**Faster every level** is measured against the starting speed rather than
compounding, so at 15% a level 5 train rides 60% faster than it started, not
75%. However fast the maths gets, the train tops out at 3000 px/s.

**Look**

| Setting | Default |
|---|---|
| **Train size** | 150 px |
| **Profile pictures** | On |
| **Bits and subs** | On |
| **Level on the engine** | On |
| **Train** / **Trim** / **Text** colours | Purple, amber, white |

## If it doesn't work

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The train is smaller than I set">
    **Train size** is capped by the height of the widget box. A 400 px train in
    a 200 px tall box draws at 200 px. Make the box taller.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Straight across runs along the bottom of my screen">
    It rides along the bottom edge of the **widget**, and the widget defaults to
    your whole overlay. Resize or move the box to put the track where you want
    it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Direction does nothing">
    **Direction** only applies to **Straight across**. In **Bounce around** the
    train picks its own way about, so the control is hidden.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A rider has no picture">
    Profile pictures are fetched from Twitch per rider. Test riders and
    anonymous contributors have no account to fetch, so they fall back to the
    first letter of the name.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A rider shows no amount">
    **Bits and subs** can only show what Twitch reported. A contribution Twitch
    only counted as an "other" total has nothing to print, so that wagon shows
    the name alone.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
