title: "Add widgets to your overlay"
description: "What a widget is, how to add one, where its settings live, and every built-in widget in one list."
---

A widget is one thing on your overlay: an alert box, your chat, a follower goal,
a clock, a logo. You add widgets in the overlay editor, drag them where you want
them, and each one has its own settings. One overlay can hold as many as you
like.

## Before you start

- You have an overlay. If not, [create one](/overlays/quickstart).
- You're on a desktop. The editor needs a bigger screen than a phone.

## Add a widget

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the overlay in the editor">
    In **Overlays**, select **Edit** on the overlay you want to change.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the widget list">
    Select **Widgets** in the editor toolbar. The **Widget library** panel opens
    with the search box ready, so you can type "goal" or "chat" straight away.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a widget">
    Select it. It lands on the canvas and shows up in the layers panel on the
    left.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Place it">
    Drag it where you want it and drag a corner to resize. The
    [editor page](/overlays/editor) has the shortcuts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set it up">
    With the widget selected, its settings appear in the panel on the right,
    under **Properties**. That's where you pick a design, colours, sounds, and
    whatever else that widget does.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Press **Ctrl+S** or select **Save**. Nothing saves on its own.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
Most widgets only show something when an event happens. To see one working
without waiting for a real follower, fire a test event: select **Demo** in the
toolbar, pick an event and select **Fire**.
</Tip>

## Every built-in widget

The list in the editor groups widgets the same way as below. Each name links to
its own page with the settings and how to set it up.

### Alerts & chat

| Widget | What it does |
|---|---|
| [Alert box](/overlays/alerts) | Follows, subs, cheers, raids, watch streaks, gift bombs and more, each with your own image, video and sound. |
| [Chat box](/overlays/chat) | Your Twitch chat on stream, with badges and 7TV, BTTV and FFZ emotes. Deleted messages disappear. |
| [Emote wall](/overlays/emote-wall) | Chat emotes fly across your stream, and follows, subs, cheers and raids set off a big burst. |
| [Emote combo](/overlays/emote-combo) | When chat spams the same emote, a counter shows it and punches up with every hit. |
| [Hype train](/overlays/hype-train) | During a hype train, a train bounces around your stream with a wagon for everyone who cheered, subbed or gifted. |

### Goals

| Widget | What it does |
|---|---|
| [Follower goal](/overlays/goals) | Your Twitch follower goal as a live progress bar. Make the goal on Twitch, watch it fill here. |
| [Sub goal](/overlays/goals) | Your Twitch sub goal, whichever kind you run: total or new, subs or sub points. |
| [Bits goal](/overlays/goals) | Your Twitch Bits goal, counting Bits or cheerers. |

### Polls & predictions

| Widget | What it does |
|---|---|
| [Poll](/overlays/polls) | Your Twitch poll with live votes, a countdown and the winner. Start the poll on Twitch. |
| [Prediction](/overlays/predictions) | Your Twitch prediction with live channel points, a countdown and the winner. Start it on Twitch. |

### Ads

| Widget | What it does |
|---|---|
| [Ads](/overlays/ads) | A heads-up before your ad break and a countdown while it runs, so viewers know when you're back. |

### Credits

| Widget | What it does |
|---|---|
| [End credits](/overlays/credits) | Rolls the names from your stream when your ending scene comes up: followers, subs, gifts, Bits, raids and more. |

### Labels

| Widget | What it does |
|---|---|
| [Label](/overlays/stream-labels) | One live value: latest follower, top cheerer, sub count, recent raids and more. |
| [Event list](/overlays/stream-labels) | A running feed of your latest follows, subs, cheers, raids and rewards. You pick which ones show. |

### Media

| Widget | What it does |
|---|---|
| [Clips](/overlays/clips-widget) | Your Twitch clips on rotation, with fields like title, creator and game around them. |
| [Image](/overlays/images-and-video) | A picture from your media library: logo, webcam frame, sponsor. GIFs move. |
| [Video](/overlays/images-and-video) | A video from your media library, looping or played once. |
| [Slideshow](/overlays/images-and-video) | Several pictures taking turns in one spot. |

### Layout

| Widget | What it does |
|---|---|
| [Text](/overlays/text-and-shapes) | Text you type, with your own font size, colour and alignment. |
| [Shape](/overlays/text-and-shapes) | Rectangle, circle, triangle, diamond, star or line, for frames, backdrops and dividers. |
| [Countdown](/overlays/time-widgets) | Counts down to a date and time: starting soon, a break, anything you schedule. |
| [Time](/overlays/time-widgets) | The date and time, in your time zone or a fixed one. |
| [Uptime](/overlays/time-widgets) | How long you've been live. Hides itself when you're offline. |

### Other: IRL

| Widget | What it does |
|---|---|
| [IRL · Speed](/overlays/irl-widgets) | How fast you're moving, from your phone's GPS. |
| [IRL · Heading](/overlays/irl-widgets) | Which way you're facing. |
| [IRL · Altitude](/overlays/irl-widgets) | How high up you are. |
| [IRL · Latitude](/overlays/irl-widgets) | Your latitude. |
| [IRL · Longitude](/overlays/irl-widgets) | Your longitude. |
| [IRL · Accuracy](/overlays/irl-widgets) | How sure the GPS is about your position. |

The IRL widgets need a GPS overlay open on your phone while you stream. The
[IRL widgets page](/overlays/irl-widgets) explains how the two fit together.

## Not finding what you need

Select **Open Library** at the top of the widget list. That's where ready-made
starters, widgets other streamers have published, and the ones you build
yourself live. See [the widget library](/overlays/widget-library).

If none of those fit, you can [build your own](/widgets/overview) with HTML, CSS
and JavaScript.

## If it doesn't work

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I added a widget and the canvas looks empty">
    Most widgets wait for an event before they show anything. An alert box with
    no alert, a poll with no poll running, or an uptime widget while you're
    offline all look empty. Fire a test from **Demo** in the toolbar, or select
    the widget in the layers panel to see its outline.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="It works in the editor but not on stream">
    Check you saved, and that the overlay's switch is on in **Overlays**. Then
    see [overlay is blank in OBS](/troubleshooting/overlay-blank).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My IRL widgets show nothing">
    They need the GPS overlay open on your phone with location allowed. Close
    that page or lose signal and they have nothing to show. See
    [IRL widgets](/overlays/irl-widgets).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
