Two widgets that don't read anything from Twitch. **Text** puts words on
screen, **Shape** draws the boxes, frames and dividers you arrange everything
else inside. Both live under **Widgets → Layout**.

## Text

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add the widget">
    Open **Widgets → Layout** and pick **Text**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write it">
    **Content** takes multiple lines. The box doesn't grow to fit, so drag an
    edge if your text is clipped.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make it readable over gameplay">
    Turn **Outline** up a little under **Outline and shadow**. A one or two
    pixel outline keeps text legible over anything.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Text** settings

| Group | Setting | Default |
|---|---|---|
| Text | **Font size** | 24 px (8 to 200) |
| Text | **Alignment** | Left |
| Outline and shadow | **Outline** | 0 px (0 to 20) |
| Outline and shadow | **Text shadow** | Off |
| Background | **Opacity** | 0% |
| Background | **Rounded corners** | 0% |
| Ticker | **Scroll the text** | Off |
| Ticker | **Speed** | 80 px/s (10 to 400) |

The background fills the whole widget rectangle, not just the words, so give
the box some room if you turn it on.

### Tickers

**Scroll the text** runs the text across the box on one line, over and over.
Good for socials, sponsors and rules.

<Note>
Scrolling collapses your line breaks into spaces and hides **Alignment**, since
neither means anything on a line that's always moving. It also respects
**reduced motion**: a viewer who's turned that on in their own system settings
sees the text sitting still.
</Note>

## Shape

Six shapes: **Rectangle**, **Circle**, **Triangle**, **Diamond**, **Star** and
**Line**.

**Fill** is **None**, **Color** or **Gradient**. A gradient takes a **From**
and **To** colour and a **Direction** in degrees, where 90° runs left to right
and 180° top to bottom.

**Outline** has its own thickness, colour and gradient, and is hidden for a
**Line**, which is already just a stroke.

### Building a frame

Set **Fill** to **None** and give the shape an outline. You get a frame with
nothing in the middle, which is how you put a border around a webcam or box off
a widget. StreamWizard sets the outline to 8 px for you when you pick **None**
with no outline set, so the shape doesn't vanish on you.

### Lines

A **Line** is always horizontal. Rotate the item to get a vertical or diagonal
one. **Thickness** sets how heavy it is and **Rounded ends** softens the caps.

<Warning>
Switching shape resets every look setting back to defaults: colours, outline,
rounding, the lot. Only size, position and rotation survive. Pick your shape
before you spend time on the colours.
</Warning>

## If it doesn't work

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="My text is cut off">
    The box clips rather than grows. Make it bigger, or turn the font size down.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Rounded corners aren't there">
    Only **Rectangle** has corners to round. The other shapes don't show the
    control.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My circle looks like an oval">
    The shape stretches to fill the box. Make the box square, or hold the
    aspect ratio while you resize.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The shape is smaller than its box">
    The outline is drawn centred on the edge, so half of it sits inside. A thick
    outline visibly shrinks the shape to keep it all in the box.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
