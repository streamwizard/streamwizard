**Settings → Preferences** has five switches. All five start on. Nothing saves
until you select **Save**.

## Sync Twitch clips automatically

Pulls new clips every five minutes while you're live, and once more when the
stream ends.

Turn it off and your library only updates when you select **Sync** yourself.
See [syncing your clips](/clips/syncing).

## Show stream stats

Shows the six stat cards at the top of your dashboard: time in ads, peak and
average viewers, time on air, new follows and new subs.

<Note>
Turning this off removes the stat cards. The chart, best hour, category table,
activity feed and recent streams all stay. The switch's own description
promises more than it currently does.
</Note>

## Post in the StreamWizard Discord when I go live

Puts your title, game and a link in the StreamWizard server the moment you go
live. The post never pings anyone.

Needs your Discord account [linked](/discord). Without that the switch is
greyed out.

## Show me as live in the StreamWizard Discord

Gives you the Live role while you stream, so you sit at the top of the member
list. It comes off when you go offline.

Also needs Discord linked. It's independent of the go-live post, so you can
have one without the other.

## Enable memes

Controls the animated transition when you switch between light and dark mode.
On gives you the full performance, off gives you a plain switch.

It changes nothing else.
