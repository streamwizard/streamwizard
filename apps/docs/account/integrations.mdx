## Twitch

Your Twitch account is how you sign in, so it can't be disconnected from here.
There'd be no account left to come back to.

The page shows which Twitch account you're signed in as, and nothing else.

## Discord

Linking is optional and gets you the go-live post, the Live role, and support
tickets that already know which account is yours. See [Discord](/discord) for
the whole picture.

**Connect Discord** links it. StreamWizard asks Discord for your account
identity and nothing else.

Once linked you get **Disconnect**, and a **Re-verify** link for when your role
hasn't turned up.

### When the role doesn't arrive

| What you see | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Hold up, you're not in the server** | Your account is linked, but roles can't be given to someone who isn't a member. Join the server and it appears on its own. |
| **Your role tripped on the way over** | Linking worked, the role grant didn't. Select **Re-verify**. |
| **Discord's the only way in right now** | Unlinking would lock you out, so it's blocked. Add another sign-in method first. |

**Re-verify** doesn't send you back to Discord. It just asks the bot to try the
role again.
