## Download your data

Select **Download my data** and confirm. You get a JSON file,
`streamwizard-data-2026-10-24.json`, straight to your device. There's no email
and no waiting.

It contains everything StreamWizard holds about you:

- Your profile and preferences
- Your Twitch connection details, without any tokens
- Your overlays, widgets and library entries
- Your clips, clip folders and sync history
- Your commands
- Your VODs, stream events and viewer counts
- Your IRL GPS tracks
- Any feedback or testimonials you left

It's your full history rather than a recent window.

<Note>
The export is all or nothing. If one part fails, you get an error rather than a
file that quietly left something out.
</Note>

## Delete your account

<Warning>
There is no undo, no grace period and no recovery. Download your data first if
you want to keep any of it.
</Warning>

Select **Delete my account** and confirm. One click, no typed confirmation.

What happens:

1. StreamWizard's access to your Twitch account is revoked, and the event
   subscriptions behind your alerts are removed.
2. Your uploaded media is deleted.
3. Your account data is deleted: clips, folders, overlays, widgets, commands,
   VODs, stream history, viewer counts, GPS tracks, preferences and your
   Twitch connection.
4. You're signed out and sent to a goodbye page.

### What this doesn't do

**It doesn't remove StreamWizard from your Twitch authorized apps.** The access
is revoked, but the entry stays in your Twitch settings until you remove it.
Go to **Twitch Settings → Connections** afterwards if you want it gone.

**It doesn't remove you from the Discord server.** Your Verified Member role is
taken off, but you stay in the server unless you leave.

**Discord ticket history is anonymised rather than deleted.** Support
conversations stay so the team keeps the thread, but your name is replaced and
the contents are cleared.

**Encrypted backups keep a copy for up to three months**, then roll off on
their own schedule. That's how backups work, not a hold on your data.
